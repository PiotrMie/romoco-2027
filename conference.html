RoMoCo

Conference

Overview

RoMoCo is an international workshop focused on robot motion and control, hosted by Poznan University of Technology. The event connects researchers working on control, planning, perception, autonomy and applications of robotic systems.

Scope

  • Control design for nonholonomic and underactuated robots
  • Optimal and constrained control design
  • Motion planning for robots and robotic systems
  • Multi-robot systems and collision avoidance
  • Localization, perception and autonomous systems
  • Machine learning methods in robotics
