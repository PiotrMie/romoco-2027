Call for Papers

The 14th International Workshop on Robot Motion and Control invites original contributions in robotics, motion planning, sensing, control and autonomous systems.

Workshop datesJune 30 – July 2, 2027
VenuePoznan University of Technology, Poznan, Poland
Submission deadlineFebruary 13, 2027
NotificationApril 23, 2027

Important Dates

  • December 2026Submissions open
  • February 13, 2027Paper submission deadline
  • April 23, 2027Notification of acceptance
  • May 29, 2027Revised paper submission deadline
  • June 30 – July 2, 2027Workshop dates
Accepted and presented papers are expected to be published in IEEE Xplore.

Scope of the Workshop

The scope includes but is not limited to:

  • Control design for nonholonomic and underactuated robots
  • Optimal and constrained control design for robotic systems
  • Multi-robot systems: networked control, collision avoidance, synchronization
  • Motion planning for robots and robotic systems
  • Control, planning and sensing issues in teleoperation and telemanipulation
  • Localization and perception for robots and autonomous systems
  • Modeling and data-based identification of robotic systems
  • Machine and robot-learning methods in motion planning and control
  • Robust and adaptive autonomous systems under uncertainty
  • Control system architectures in robotics
  • Safety issues of robotic systems
  • Human-robot cooperative systems
  • Robotic cars and autonomous vehicles
  • Sensors, devices and tools for robots
  • Applications of robotics (agriculture, space, underwater, air, medicine, civil engineering and services, manufacturing, etc.)
  • Social, educational, and economic aspects of robotics
